Elina's ICU Pocket Guide

Core critical care formulas, normal values, and interpretation rules, each with a live calculator. Change any number to see the result update.

A study aid built from widely taught physiology. Normal ranges vary by lab and textbook, so always follow your program's references and your facility's protocols at the bedside.

Formulas use mg/dL for chemistry, mmHg for pressures, and assume sea-level barometric pressure (760 mmHg) unless you change it. Values shown on first load are example numbers, not patient data.
