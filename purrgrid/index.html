Tap a square to mark it. Switch to Cat to place one.
How to play
  • Place exactly one cat in every row, every column, and every colored area.
  • Cats need space: no two cats may touch, not even at the corners.
  • Mark mode drops an X on squares you've ruled out. Drag to mark several.
  • Cat mode places a cat. A wrong cat costs a heart. Lose all 3 and the puzzle resets.
  • Every puzzle has exactly one solution, so it can always be solved by logic.
