      //About

      Lori Lori is an independent artist and the creator behind Maximum Reality, a self-built cyberpunk universe of music, games, stories, and art. Her songs explore signals, glitches, and the blurry line between people and the programs they make.

      Every track is written, produced, and released independently on the Maximum Reality label, made in the downtime between long hauls on the road. Two cats, Azul and Mochkil, supervise operations.
