      //About

      Lori Lori is the electronic music identity of Lori Sifuentes, creator of Maximum Reality. An independent artist, songwriter, and producer, Lori creates original synthwave, glitchpop, techno, IDM/experimental, and electronic music inspired by cyberpunk worlds, digital signals, human experience, and the possibilities beyond everyday reality.

      Her music is part of Maximum Reality, an interconnected creative universe of free browser games, interactive stories, original characters, digital art, and music. Every project is another doorway into the same imaginative world. You can start anywhere.

      Written and developed independently between long-haul trucking routes across America, Lori's songs blend personal storytelling with electronic experimentation, vocoders, chiptune melodies, and glitch-driven sound. Her music is released under the Maximum Reality label.

      Discover music from Lori Lori on Spotify, YouTube, and SoundCloud. Explore the wider universe at MaximumReality.xyz.
